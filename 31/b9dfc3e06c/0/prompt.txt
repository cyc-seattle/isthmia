    - Staff, Volunteers and Instructors sections are gated on a `staff` group mirrored from `all@`.
    - The Roster section is gated on a `families` group.
    - Anyone in neither group gets the help page, which points at `<contact-inbox>`. No contact inbox address appears anywhere in the repo, so that placeholder needs filling.
  - **How staff get into Authentik.** The new `community-sync` job reads `all@` through the gsuite package's Directory client. Its member listing needs a small extension to include members of nested groups. Authentik's Google Workspace provider only pushes users from Authentik to Google, so it can't do this.
  - **Staff rollout.** Authentik goes up first, then a temporary `preview.cycsail.team` where staff confirm sign-in and their sections. Only then does `cycsail.team` switch over, followed by removing oauth2-proxy, its cookie secret, `substrateSelfSign`, and the old delegation.

Directus emails are unique, so their family login would collide with their staff user and fail. The move is one change to `ungoodUser`, with `google` kept on until that login is verified.
  - **Team membership is now pure Directus rules.** I dropped all the roster tables. `clubspot` adds three one-to-many alias fields: `programs.classes`, `classes.registration_entries` and `registrations.registration_entries`. The rules are keyed on `$CURRENT_USER.email`.
    - A shared family email needs no mapping, because `_eq` matches every row that carries it.
    - The only mapping I kept is `people.login_email`, a trimmed and lowercased copy of `people.email` written by the sync. `_eq` is case-sensitive, and `_icontains` is a substring match, so it isn't safe for identity. Authentik's claim mapping lowercases the email on the Directus side, so both sides of the comparison match.
    - "Adult" is `date_of_birth _lte $NOW(-18 years)`, so a missing date of birth counts as a minor with no special case.
    - The Community role gets three policies: `names`, `contacts` and `family`. This needs a new `DirectusPolicy` resource and `presets` support on `DirectusPermissionRule`.
  - **Newest opt-in answer wins.** `people.share_contact_updated_at` records when the answer was given. On the portal side, a permission preset sets it to `$NOW`, so the client can never set it. On the Clubspot side, the answer's time is its registration's `registered_at`, because `custom_field_responses` has no timestamp of its own. `planPromotedFields` gets a newest-wins mode for this one field.
